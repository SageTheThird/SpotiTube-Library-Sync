Personal music-library automation

BeatBridger

BeatBridger synchronizes liked music between YouTube, YouTube Music, and Spotify.

What it does

  • Reads liked tracks and videos from connected accounts.
  • Matches equivalent releases across music services.
  • Adds missing likes while keeping resumable local sync history.

How data is handled

The application is self-hosted. OAuth credentials, tokens, sync plans, and history remain on the operator's machine. BeatBridger does not sell user data or operate a hosted user-data service.
