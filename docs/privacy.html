BeatBridger

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 12, 2026

Data accessed

BeatBridger may access YouTube and YouTube Music library information, including liked videos or tracks and basic channel information, plus Spotify liked-track information. It also processes the OAuth credentials needed to perform actions authorized by the user.

How data is used

Data is used only to compare connected music libraries, find equivalent tracks, add missing likes, resume interrupted synchronization, and report operational success or failure.

Storage and retention

BeatBridger is self-hosted. Tokens, cached search results, synchronization plans, and history are stored locally on the operator's machine and retained until the operator deletes them. The project does not run a central database or hosted user-data service.

Sharing

Data is sent only to Google/YouTube and Spotify as required to perform the requested synchronization. If the operator enables Discord notifications, limited operational summaries such as sync direction, status, and item counts may be sent to the configured Discord webhook. Personal data is not sold.

Security and control

Users can revoke Google access from their Google Account security settings and Spotify access from their Spotify application settings. The operator can remove locally stored data by deleting BeatBridger's authentication, cache, plan, synchronization, export, and log files.
